Ownership and buying

Clean title, honest answers

The land is held under a single freehold certificate (SHM, Hak Milik). You can ask for a copy on WhatsApp and have your own notary check it with the land office before you commit to anything.

Can a foreigner buy this land?

Indonesian citizens can take over the freehold title directly. Foreign buyers usually purchase through a foreign-owned company (PT PMA), with the title converted to a Right to Build (HGB), or under a Right to Use (Hak Pakai) with a residence permit. A licensed notary (PPAT) handles the transfer.

What does pink zone mean?

The plot sits in a zone where both residential and commercial buildings are permitted. Your notary or architect can confirm the current zoning letter for your project.

Who am I dealing with?

The owners directly. No broker in between, so no agent fee and no telephone game.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Can I pay in USD or EUR?

Yes. Payment is made by bank wire transfer, preferably in EUR or USD.

Who pays the notary fees and taxes?

Each side pays its own tax, and we share the notary. We pay the seller's income tax (2.5%). The buyer pays the acquisition tax (BPHTB, 5%) and the land office registration. The notary fee, usually 0.5 to 1% of the price, is split 50/50. As a buyer, budget around 5 to 6% on top of the price.

What is the payment schedule?

Payment is made in full at the signing of the sale deed (AJB) in front of the notary (PPAT). The notary checks the certificate with the land office beforehand and registers the transfer to your name afterwards, so both sides are protected: you pay only against a verified title, and we hand over the title only against payment.

Can I buy without travelling to Bali?

Yes. The purchase can be arranged remotely with the notary.

How long does the sale take?

The transaction can be completed with no delays on our side.

Can I start building right away?

The land is in the pink zone and is ready to build on.

How far is it from the airport and Sanur?

About 10 minutes to Semarapura, the main town of Klungkung, 45 minutes to Sanur and 1 hour to the airport.
